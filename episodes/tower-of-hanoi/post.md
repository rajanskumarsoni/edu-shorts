TITLE:
Will the Universe End When This Puzzle is Solved? ⏳ Tower of Hanoi & Recursion Explained

DESCRIPTION:
An ancient legend says that when monks finish moving 64 golden disks on three diamond needles, the universe will end! But how long does it actually take? Here is the mind-bending math of the Tower of Hanoi, why brute force fails, and how computer science solves it in 3 lines using Recursion.

FACTS TO VERIFY BEFORE PUBLISHING:
- Invented by French mathematician Édouard Lucas in 1883
- Formula for optimal moves: 2^n - 1
- For 64 disks: 2^64 - 1 = 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 moves
- At 1 move/second: ~585 billion years (42x older than the 13.8 billion year universe)
