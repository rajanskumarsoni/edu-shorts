TITLE:
How Does an Air Conditioner Work? The Heat-Transfer Loop

DESCRIPTION:
A split AC does not bring cold air from outside. Follow the refrigerant as it absorbs heat indoors, carries it through the system, and releases it outdoors.

HASHTAGS:
#AirConditioner #HowItWorks #Physics #HVAC #Shorts

FACTS TO VERIFY BEFORE PUBLISHING:
- A split AC uses an indoor evaporator and an outdoor condenser connected by refrigerant piping.
- The compressor raises refrigerant pressure and temperature; the expansion device lowers pressure before the refrigerant returns indoors.
